# Learner rules & phase outcomes

From **Chapter 2 — AI Instructions**.

---

## Learner rules

1. **Explore AI tools and understand how they work**  
   So you can use them ethically and reduce potential biases.

2. **Reflect on your problem before prompting**  
   This leads to clearer, more detailed, and more relevant prompts and accurate vocabulary.

3. **Check, review, question, and test AI output**  
   Make it a habit to systematically validate anything generated by AI.

4. **Always seek peer review**  
   Don’t rely only on your own validation.

---

## Phase outcomes

- Develop both **general-purpose** and **domain-specific** prompting skills.
- Boost productivity through **effective use of AI tools**.
- Keep strengthening **computational thinking**, **problem-solving**, **adaptability**, and **collaboration**.

---

## Comments from the chapter

- You’ll face situations (exams, evaluations, etc.) where you must **demonstrate real understanding**. Keep building technical and interpersonal skills.
- **Explaining your reasoning and debating with peers** often reveals gaps. Make peer learning a priority.
- AI often **lacks your specific context** and gives generic answers. Peers who share your environment can give more relevant, accurate insights.
- AI tends to give the **most likely answer**; peers can offer **alternative perspectives and nuance**. Use them as a quality checkpoint.

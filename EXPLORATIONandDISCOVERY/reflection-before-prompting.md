# Reflection before prompting

Use this checklist **before** you send a prompt to an AI tool. It supports the chapter rule: *reflect on your problem before prompting*.

---

## 1. Clarify the problem

- [ ] What exactly am I trying to achieve? (one sentence)
- [ ] What part is repetitive or tedious vs. what I need to think through myself?
- [ ] Do I have the right vocabulary to describe it? (If not, look up one or two terms first.)

---

## 2. Scope the request

- [ ] Am I asking for a small, testable step or for “the whole thing”?
- [ ] Could I explain the expected input and output to a peer?
- [ ] Is this something I’m allowed to use AI for in this context (e.g. activity / exam rules)?

---

## 3. Plan for validation

- [ ] How will I **test** the result (run it, try examples, edge cases)?
- [ ] Who can I **review** it with (peer, eval partner)?
- [ ] Can I **explain** the result in my own words and take responsibility for it?

---

## 4. After you get a response

- [ ] Run / try the suggestion; don’t assume it’s correct.
- [ ] Question anything that’s unclear or that you couldn’t justify in an evaluation.
- [ ] Refine with a peer if possible — catch bugs and misunderstandings together.

---

**Reminder:** Only use AI-generated content that you fully understand and can take responsibility for.
